HEADS UP

Windows might warn you. Here's why.

SysPeek isn't code-signed yet. Signing certificates cost money, so for now Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time you open it. That's the standard warning for new, unsigned apps, not a sign that anything is wrong.
